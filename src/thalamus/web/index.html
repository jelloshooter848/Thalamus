    1. Install Tailscale on this PC and on your phone (tailscale.com/download) and sign in to the same account on both.
    2. Turn on phone access below, choose a passcode, and save.
    3. Close the THALAMUS window and double-click the launcher again.

    Email accounts

    THALAMUS reads your email to tell you what matters. It's read-only: it never sends, moves, deletes or marks anything as read. Passwords are kept in Windows Credential Manager (or a private file), never in .env.

      1. Go to Azure portal → App registrations (sign in with your Microsoft account) and click New registration.
      2. Name it "THALAMUS". Under Supported account types choose Accounts in any organizational directory and personal Microsoft accounts. Leave Redirect URI empty. Click Register.
      3. Open Authentication, set Allow public client flows to Yes, and save.
      4. Open API permissions → Add a permission → APIs my organization uses, search Office 365 Exchange Online, choose Delegated → IMAP.AccessAsUser.All, and add it.
      5. Copy the Application (client) ID from Overview into the box above.

      Phone notifications

      Get an alert on your phone when an important email arrives, through the free ntfy app.

      1. Install ntfy from the App Store (or Google Play).
      2. Turn notifications on below. A private topic name is created for you.
      3. In ntfy, tap +, enter the topic name exactly, and tap Subscribe.
      4. Click Send test.

      Alerts include the sender and subject and go through ntfy.sh. Anyone who knows the topic name could subscribe to it, so keep it private. Click "new topic" to change it.
